# Problem Statement 26091

**Title:** AI-Driven Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Structuring Assistant for Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs

## Description
The government actively promotes the economic empowerment of marginalized communities by providing concessional credit for income-generating activities. Under various schemes, beneficiaries are required to contribute a small margin money fraction (typically 10% of the total project cost), while the State Channelizing agencies (SCAs) provide the remaining 90% as a concessional loan.

These loans are categorized into specific tiers:
- **Micro Finance Scheme:** For small units with a project cost up to ₹1.40 lakh (6.5% interest, 3 years tenure, 3 months moratorium).
- **Term Loan Scheme:** For projects up to ₹50 lakh (8% interest, 7 years tenure, 6 months moratorium).

Many micro-entrepreneurs struggle because they do not understand whether their business idea is viable in their specific local geography, nor do they know how to properly structure their project cost and requested loan amount based on the 10% margin they possess.

## Objective
Build a platform that asks the user for their location, business category, and available 10% margin, and outputs:
1. **Business Viability Analysis:** A hyper-local breakdown of market reach, competitors, SWOT, threats, and pricing.
2. **Financial Structuring Plan:** Calculation of the 100% project cost, the 90% loan amount, automatic routing to the correct scheme (Micro or Term), and a quarterly EMI/Moratorium repayment schedule.
